# Privacy notes

## What is stored

Emotion Wheel stores the emotion records you create, optional reflection fields, optional notes, the capture settings you choose, and your selected colour theme. This information is stored in your browser's local storage on the current device.

## Anonymous usage statistics

Emotion Wheel uses GoatCounter to count visits to its pages. When a page loads, your browser requests a small script from `gc.zgo.at` and sends a pageview to `emotionwheel.goatcounter.com`.

GoatCounter may process the page path and referrer, browser and operating-system category, language, screen width, and an approximate country derived from the network address. GoatCounter says that it does not store IP addresses, full browser user-agent strings, or tracking identifiers, and does not place cookies or use local storage in your browser. Information used to recognise repeat visits is held temporarily in memory for up to eight hours.

The statistics do not include your emotion selections, reflection answers, intensity values, physical sensations, or notes. GoatCounter is operated from Ireland and its hosted service stores data on servers in Finland and Germany. You can prevent these statistics by blocking `goatcounter.com` and `gc.zgo.at`, including with a content blocker.

Read [GoatCounter's privacy information](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

## What is not collected

Emotion Wheel does not require an account or use tracking cookies. Your saved records are kept locally unless you explicitly choose an export or the optional Google Drive connection. It does not track actions within an entry.

## When data leaves your browser

Anonymous pageview information is sent automatically as described above. Your saved emotion content leaves your browser only when you choose to export a CSV file, save a JSON backup, save or sync data to Google Drive, share a chart or table, or copy information to the clipboard. Check the destination and the contents before sharing, particularly if your note includes sensitive information.

## Optional Google Drive preview

Google Drive is optional. Google sign-in scripts load when you open Maintenance or a shared Drive link. Signing in requires choosing Connect Google Drive. Google handles account selection and consent. The app requests access to files it creates or you explicitly select, rather than all your Drive files. Google also supplies your verified email address for the connected-account label. That label stays in memory. The share owner is displayed separately using Drive file metadata.

Saving a copy uploads the complete JSON backup, including notes, recorded fields, tags, rating scale and capture settings, to your selected Google account's Drive. Saving current updates one file in the Emotion Wheel folder; separate dated backups go in its Backups folder. Extra backups above the chosen limit (five by default) are listed oldest first and moved to Trash only after confirmation. The current file is kept; later recordings are not uploaded automatically unless you separately enable two-way sync. Sync updates one owned Drive file and merges its changes into your local log while the page is open and connected. A full common baseline and a recovery snapshot are stored in this browser. Sync deletion markers contain record IDs; older recovery copies and Drive revisions can still retain deleted records. Sync pauses on conflicts, failures, reload or disconnect. Choose named recipients and give them Viewer access in the app; email notification is optional. Anyone who retrieves a file can retain their own copy; removing their Drive access does not remove copies already downloaded.

A sharing link contains only a file ID, without a token or emotion records. Shared backups open read-only in the existing Logs and Charts, separately from your personal records and settings. Entry and log edits are disabled while viewing them. The browser remembers only shared file IDs and your local labels; it does not persist downloaded shared records. Selecting a shared file checks access again. Removing a file from the list removes its local bookmark without deleting the Drive file. Switching to local data, disconnecting or closing the page discards downloaded shared records. Access tokens and the connected-account label stay in memory and are cleared on disconnect. Your local records and Drive files are retained. Disconnecting does not revoke the app’s Google permission; manage that separately in your Google account.

## Keeping control of your data

Use Maintenance to save a JSON backup, load a previous backup, or clear the current log. Clearing the log removes the app's stored records from the current browser. Clearing site data in the browser also removes the records and theme preference, so save a backup first if you want to keep them.

## Important limitation

This is a personal reflection tool, not a medical service, emergency service, or secure clinical record. Avoid storing information you would not be comfortable keeping in local browser storage on the device you are using.
