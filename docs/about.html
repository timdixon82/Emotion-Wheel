About Emotion Wheel

Emotion Wheel is an accessible, single-page tool for recording and reflecting on emotions in your own browser. It is designed to make everyday entry simple while providing richer review tools for spotting patterns.

Recording

Record an emotion from the inner, middle or outer circle. Phase 1 supports simple habit-building, while Phase 2, Phase 3 and Custom modes add optional reflection information and notes.

Reviewing

Logs, tallies, written insights, accessible charts and matching tables help you review information by day, week, month or all time.

Managing your information

Your entries are saved in this browser. My Data provides exports and backups, plus optional Google Drive saving, two-way sync and backup restore. You can share your current Drive file with named viewers and remove their access later in Google Drive. Data Shared with Me lets you switch between other people’s read-only logs and charts. No Google account is needed for local use. Read Help and the Privacy notice before connecting or sharing.
