New features are here!

Back up your records before updating.

Save an original backup to this device or Google Drive, then choose “Backup saved — update”. Your saved ratings will stay the same. You can still view and export records while you wait, but recording, editing and loading backups are paused.

What’s new

  • More ways to capture context: select multiple tags, create your own tags and record how full your emotion bucket feels. Choose ratings out of 5 or 10 in Advanced settings.
  • Improved charts to explore your most recorded tags and how emotions relate to tags and bucket levels.
  • Improved Logs and filters: combine dates, search, multiple emotions, tags, bucket levels, expected and actual intensity, and alignment.
  • Choose Custom on Charts to use the same filters for your analysis.

These extra capture fields are optional. Your existing records and older backups remain supported.

Record your emotions and explore patterns over time.

Capture settings — Phase 1: Emotion only

Choose how much detail to record.

  • Phase 1: Record just the emotion.
  • Phase 2: Also record how strong the emotion feels, whether it fits the situation and how your body feels.
  • Phase 3: Everything in Phase 2, plus tags, such as Work or Family, and how full your emotional bucket feels.
  • Custom: Choose which questions to include.
Questions to include
Advanced settings — ratings out of 5

Choose ratings out of 5 or 10 for emotion strength and your emotional bucket. This applies whichever phase you use.

If you have saved records, you’ll be asked to save a backup and confirm before their ratings are changed.

Change rating scale

This changes all saved emotion strength and bucket ratings. Anything you did not record stays Not recorded.
